Why Volunteer with Harvard Alumni in Tech?

Gain Valuable Experience

Work on impactful projects showcasing leadership, creativity, and technical skills. Stand out to future employers by demonstrating your active involvement and leadership within the Harvard Alumni in Tech community.

Expand Your Network

Connect with Harvard alumni and tech professionals for mentorship, collaboration, and career opportunities.

Make a Difference

Be a part of something bigger and contribute your time and talent to initiatives that drive innovation, foster community engagement, and support the responsible growth of technology.

Other Benefits

Discounts on select Harvard University online courses.
