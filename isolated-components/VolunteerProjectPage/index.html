Chapter launch team — Toronto

~3 hrs / week

Help stand up our next city chapter: find a venue, rally the first 30 alumni, and run the launch mixer.

Toronto has more than 400 Harvard alumni working in technology and no chapter to bring them together. This project takes the city from a mailing list to a first real event.

What you'll do

  • Scout and book a venue for a 30–50 person evening mixer.
  • Draft the invitation and reach Toronto-area alumni through the newsletter.
  • Recruit two or three co-organizers to keep the chapter running after launch.
  • Run the launch mixer, including a short welcome and a round of introductions.

What we're looking for

You don't need event-management experience — you need to be organized, comfortable emailing strangers, and based in or near Toronto.
